§ 03

Services

A specialist works the problem with you, on the line, until it plays — or until you have a straight answer about what the vehicle needs next.

What a call actually gets you

No scripts read at you — and an honest answer if it needs a shop.

Activation & re-activation

New vehicle, used vehicle, or a trial that never switched on. We walk through radio ID lookup, send the refresh, and stay on the line until channels appear.

Signal & reception diagnosis

We isolate whether it’s the antenna, the cable, the mount, the location or the receiver — so you don’t pay a shop to find out.

Channel lineup restore

Channels vanished after an update or a plan change? We check what your package should carry and rebuild the lineup on the receiver.

Subscription & billing guidance

Understand what you’re on, what renews when, and how to move service to a different car — explained in plain English.

Pairing & head-unit setup

Bluetooth handoff, steering-wheel controls, presets, and factory head units that lost their settings after a battery change.

Second-opinion troubleshooting

Already been round the loop elsewhere? Call and we’ll tell you what’s actually wrong — even if the answer is “this one needs hardware.”
